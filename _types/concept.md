# concept

A unit of knowledge in a knowledge base — the conceptual material LKF was first
written for. It may describe a tangible asset (a table, an API), an abstract
idea (a metric, a business process), or anything between.

It adds no fields of its own. Declaring `type: concept` is a semantic claim
rather than a structural one: *this is knowledge*, as against a task, a record,
or a Bundle's own manifest. A type whose whole content is its name is still
worth having when the name is what a reader needs.
